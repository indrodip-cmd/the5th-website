You know more than you think. This workbook helps you turn it into a product people will pay for. You are good at what you do. But your income stops the moment you stop working, and the idea of building something you own feels like a maze of tech, courses and advice you never finish. Expertise to Income is a 9-chapter, 104-page workbook that walks you from "I have knowledge" to a finished digital product, a clear offer and a 7-day launch plan, written in your own words. You read a short chapter, then you write on the page. Every chapter ends with something finished, not just ideas. What you will do, chapter by chapter
  • Understand your why. A 3-layer method that finds the real reason behind your business, so you keep going on slow days.
  • Diagnose where you are. Map how you earn today, where money leaks, and an expertise inventory of what you already know that others would pay to learn.
  • Build an asset mindset. See the difference between trading time and owning an asset, and plan a product ladder from a $7 entry product upward.
  • Find your perfect audience. The One Person method: who you serve, where they spend time, and the problems they think about at midnight.
  • Design your first digital product. Choose the fastest format, price it with confidence, and say what it does in one sentence.
  • Create a simple offer. Stack your product, bonuses and guarantee into an offer, then write a one-page description.
  • Get found without ads. Use the Story, Lesson, CTA formula and a 30-day content map with five posts drafted.
  • Launch in seven days. Set up your profile and selling platform step by step, with copy-and-personalise message scripts.
  • Keep going when it gets quiet. A plan for rejection, doubt and slow days, plus a 5-minute daily reset.
This workbook is for you if
  • You are an experienced professional, coach, consultant, teacher, therapist or creator who wants income that does not depend on your hours.
  • You suspect your knowledge is "too basic" to sell, but people keep asking you for advice.
  • You have started courses and quit halfway because they felt overwhelming.
  • You do not know what to sell, who to sell it to, or what to charge.
  • You are not "techy" and want every tool explained in plain English.
