Refund Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains when refunds are available for The5th Global Consulting services and products. The terms below are designed to make the process predictable and prevent duplicate claims; they do not remove any rights you have under applicable consumer law.

For the India ₹199 Roadmap Strategy Session, the advertised “200%” guarantee means ₹400 total back: the ₹199 paid plus ₹201 extra. It does not mean ₹400 in addition to your original payment.

1. India Roadmap Strategy Session (₹199)

Session delivery: Your ₹199 payment reserves one 60-minute strategy session with Indrodip Ghosh and Manisha Satpute. If The5th cancels and cannot offer a reasonable replacement time, you may choose a reschedule or a full refund of ₹199. If you need to move your appointment, contact us at least 24 hours in advance; one reschedule is allowed, subject to availability.

₹400 total satisfaction guarantee: If you attend the session and believe the promised review of your offer, positioning, buyer, and next steps was not delivered, email us within 7 calendar days after the session (or, if later, 7 days after receiving the written audit). Include the booking email, session date, and a short explanation of what promised part was missing. We acknowledge consumer complaints within 48 hours and aim to decide guarantee requests within 5 business days. If eligible, we will return ₹400 total for that booking: ₹199 plus ₹201.

  • One guarantee claim per customer and paid booking. Multiple email addresses, duplicate bookings, or repeat submissions do not create additional payouts.
  • The total amount paid back under this guarantee cannot exceed ₹400 for the same booking. A payment reversal, chargeback, or other refund already received for that payment is counted toward the same total; there is no double recovery.
  • The guarantee is about delivery of the session described on the page. It is not a promise of revenue, clients, or business results.
  • If you do not attend, the satisfaction guarantee does not apply. Contact us promptly if there was an emergency or a booking/technical issue; we will review it fairly. This does not limit remedies that may be required by law.

To request the guarantee, email indrodip@10kroadmap.org with the subject “India session refund”, your booking email and date, and the short explanation above. Approved payments are returned to the original payment method where possible. Processing times depend on your bank or payment provider.
