## Before you click Publish
1. Open the EPUB in **Kindle Previewer** (free from Amazon) and in KDP's online previewer. Check the diagrams and tables on phone, tablet and e-ink sizes.
2. Order **one proof copy** of the paperback. It is the only real cost (printing plus shipping) and the only way to see the actual print quality.
3. Read the claims in the book against what you can prove: "20+ clients", "$10K months" and the chapter titled "Mindset for Consistent $10K Months" are your own statements, and Amazon may ask for support if a reader reports them. This edition already removes the free-call offer, the email-gated bonuses and the "$15M in sales" line from the About page.
4. Complete the tax interview truthfully (you will need your tax details as the account holder).
5. Reviews: ask real readers for honest reviews. Never buy or trade reviews; Amazon removes them and can suspend accounts.
