The 10K Roadmap Workbook

Stop selling your time. Start building an asset.

You know a lot. You may even be good enough that people already ask you for advice. But if your income stops the moment you stop working, you do not own a business. You own a job with extra steps.

Expertise to Income is a step-by-step workbook that takes you from what you know to a finished digital product, a simple offer and a 7-day launch plan. Read a short chapter, then write on the page. No team. No ads. No tech skills.

  • Find the reason that keeps you going on slow days
  • Choose the one person you serve
  • Design, price and package your first product
  • Write an offer with a guarantee that is easy to say yes to
  • Launch in seven days with scripts you can copy
