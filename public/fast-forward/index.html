The Real Problem

You tell people they are worthy. Then you quote your own price and your voice shrinks.

It is rarely a strategy problem. It is a blockage problem sitting on top of a system problem, and fixing only one is why most programs fail healers and coaches.

“Who am I to charge that much?”

What is going on

This is the worth block. You can hold space for a stranger's pain and still flinch when you say your own price out loud.

What it costs you

You undercharge, discount on the call and attract people who value your work less than you do.

What we do about it

We start with an income-ceiling audit to find where your number really stops, then build the identity of a paid expert and price for the result you create.

“It feels wrong to charge for healing.”

What is going on

This is the money story. Somewhere you learned that helping and earning cannot sit together, so you give the work away or apologise for the invoice.

What it costs you

You burn out serving for little, and the people who most need you cannot rely on you being around.

What we do about it

We rewrite the money story behind it and reframe the exchange, then put a fair, confident price on a clearly defined transformation.

“I give too much away for free.”

What is going on

This is the boundary block. Free calls, free advice and open-ended sessions feel kind, but they train people to expect your time at no cost.

What it costs you

Your calendar fills with people who never buy, and the paying work gets what is left of you.

What we do about it

We design one structured offer with a clear start and finish, set the boundaries into it and run sales conversations that qualify instead of give away.

“I am afraid of being seen.”

What is going on

This is the visibility block. You can show up for clients in private, but posting, reaching out and telling people what you do feels exposed.

What it costs you

Your best work stays invisible, so the right people never find you.

What we do about it

We build a content engine and a warm-outreach system in small steps, in your voice, so being seen becomes a routine and not a performance.

“I have tried before and it did not stick.”

What is going on

This is the consistency block. You start strong, then the doubt, the job or the overwhelm pulls you back to the old pattern.

What it costs you

Each restart costs confidence, and a pile of unfinished funnels and courses proves the doubt right.

What we do about it

We take the build work off your plate, agree a weekly plan that fits your life and review progress against a milestone every phase, so momentum does not depend on willpower.

“I do not know what to sell.”

What is going on

This is the clarity block. You are good at many things and cannot see which one people would pay for.

What it costs you

You stay in planning, or build something nobody asked for.

What we do about it

Phases one and two audit your experience, audience and numbers, then shape one transformation-first offer around your proven results.
