The Guarantee, In Writing

We are not asking you to buy another coaching program.

We commit to work alongside you to build a business capable of replacing your employment income. If you do the agreed work and the agreed income target is not reached by the agreed date, you get 100% of your Program Fee back.

How The Guarantee Works
01
We set your baseline

You provide documents showing your current employment income. Your income target is set from that record, not from an estimate.

02
We agree the target and the date

One income measure, one monthly target and a measurement date between 6 and 12 months from your start. Both parties initial it before you begin.

03
You do the agreed work

Measurable standards for sessions, assignments and implementation, set realistically around your job. A shared log records progress, and you can review it.

04
If the target is missed, you are refunded

Submit a written claim with income evidence. If you met the agreed requirements and the target was not achieved, the Program Fee is refunded in full.

What we commit to

  • Personalised strategic guidance based on your goals, skills and circumstances
  • Coaching sessions at times arranged around your availability
  • A clear implementation direction after every session
  • Honest feedback and accurate progress records
  • Replies within the response times agreed in writing
  • Honouring the guarantee, including refunding on time when it applies

What we ask of you

  • Attend scheduled sessions, or reschedule with notice
  • Complete the agreed assignments by the agreed dates
  • Implement the agreed strategy and weekly plan
  • Communicate challenges and progress honestly
  • Provide accurate income documents, then evidence at the end
  • Keep up the agreed payments
