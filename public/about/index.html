06The founder
“I wasn't born into abundance. I built it.”

I grew up in a lower-middle-class family in India, where every rupee was counted twice. My parents broke into their savings to put me through college. No wealthy relatives. No business network. No shortcuts.

A degree was not enough. What followed were years of trying, stumbling and starting again, until I became obsessed with business, psychology, marketing, sales and how people really make decisions. Today I have advised Fortune 500 executives, billion-dollar companies, celebrity coaches and entrepreneurs around the world.

But the work I am proudest of is helping ordinary people build businesses they never believed were possible. That is the standard The5th is built to.
