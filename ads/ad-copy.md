# Roadmap Session — ad copy

8 concepts for `/roadmap-session`. Campaign setup: one campaign, one
Advantage+ ad set (US, minimum age 25 control, age suggestion 35–65+, gender
suggestion women), optimize for Lead, $50/day. UTM template on every ad:
`utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.name}}&utm_term={{adset.name}}`.

No ad below pairs a dollar figure with a timeframe, states age, implies a
guarantee tied to income, uses a banned word (guaranteed/secret/loophole/
passive income/effortless), or uses clinical/therapy language. Every line
was checked against `FUNNEL_BRIEF.md` section 4 individually — see the
compliance note under each concept.

---

## 1. Money mindset

**Hook:** "You know your worth. Your pricing doesn't."

**Primary text:**
Most coaches and consultants don't lose clients because their work isn't good enough. They lose them because of a number they never said out loud, and the belief behind it.

On a free 60-minute Roadmap Session, we look at what's actually behind your pricing, not just the number itself. You leave with your offer, your buyer, and your next 100 days. No pitch.

**Headline:** A Free Session to Find the Belief Behind Your Pricing

**CTA:** Learn More

**Format:** Single image (founder photo) or short static carousel

**Compliance note:** "Money mindset" is the brief's allowed term (not "trauma" or "heal"); no dollar figure, no deadline, no guarantee.

## Testimonial cut notes (using existing on-page video testimonials)

1. **Cut source:** the on-page video testimonials used in the "Client reviews after their Roadmap Session" section of `/roadmap-session` (`components/VideoWall.tsx`, first 2–3 clips).
   **Cut direction:** 15–20 second cut of the client's unscripted reaction, ending on their strongest line. Overlay "Results are not typical. Results vary." for the full duration of any on-screen number. **Pending consent** per client before use in a paid ad (separate from on-page display).

2. **Cut source:** a mid-length clip where the client specifically mentions having tried something else first.
   **Cut direction:** Open on that line ("I'd already tried X before this"), it's the strongest pattern-interrupt for the skeptical-buyer audience. **Pending consent.**

3. **Cut source:** any clip where the client describes a specific, concrete change (not a dollar figure) — e.g., "I could finally say what I do in one sentence."
   **Cut direction:** Use as a 10-second bumper before the founder Reel scripts above, so the ad opens with proof before the founder's voice. **Pending consent.**

## AI-as-tool angle

Not used in this set of 8. The brief caps it at "at most 1 of 8, reassurance only," and none of the 8 concepts above needed it to cover the required angles (money mindset, healing+business, positioning, expertise packaging, bottleneck, social proof, new entrepreneurs/homemakers, authors/speakers). If the founder wants a 9th concept testing the AI angle later, it should say only that The5th AI exists as a supporting tool, never imply AI teaches the core method, and never sit next to any income claim — all per section 2 of the brief.
